Getting here

Subway Express Bus Terminal Station (Lines 3/7/9), Exit G2 → connects to the Some Sevit parking lot

Bus Jamsu Bridge 405, 740 · Banpo Bridge south end 143, 401, 406, 8340

By car
From Jamsil: cross Banpo Bridge → enter the parking lot after 150 m
From the airport: towards the National Cemetery → Banpo IC → Isu overpass → 9 o'clock at the Jamsu Bridge roundabout
From Gangbuk: via Jamsu Bridge → parking lot, or Hannam Bridge → cross Banpo Bridge → lot after 150 m
From Gangnam: overpass at the St. Mary's Hospital intersection → 9 o'clock at the Jamsu Bridge roundabout

* Access is via Jamsu Bridge, not Banpo Bridge.
